# Asset provenance

The inline mark in `lib/client.js` reproduces the geometry and palette of
`care/care/web_assets/maestro-mark.svg`. That asset was derived from the
MAESTRO landing-page identity in the owner-authorized local checkout
`care-landing` (recorded landing commit: `19267197d4c81f43d519fd726db3232870f3be29`).

No DeepSeek artwork is copied into this package. The whale is replaced through
the public brand slots declared by the upstream client.
